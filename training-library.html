Background

Why this library exists

A strong SAP GRC analyst can still lose an interview to a resume that reads generic, or freeze on a follow-up question a hiring manager expected them to have a real answer for. The Training Library exists to close that gap — every document in it is built from actual SAP security, GRC, and controls hiring patterns, not repurposed general career advice, so what you say in an interview matches what the person across the table is actually screening for.
