Standalone judgment-first programme · pairs with Mastery

Why it exists: Most people who land their first SAP controls or audit role don't get production system access on day one. Why three, not one

Each app teaches a different half of the job

One course can't train judgment, execution, and job placement equally well without going shallow on all three. So the register is split on purpose — each programme is deep in exactly one thing.

The problem with one course

Controls work is three different skills wearing one job title

Reading evidence and forming a judgment is a different muscle from configuring a fix in SAP, which is a different muscle again from getting a recruiter to say yes. Most training compresses all three into one syllabus and trains none of them well.

The register's answer

Depth over breadth, at every stage

No-Access Track goes deep on judgment with real evidence. Mastery Console goes deep on execution with live labs. The Training Library goes deep on placement with career intelligence built specifically for SAP GRC roles.

What this means for you

Start where you are, not where a syllabus assumes

Already comfortable reading evidence? Start at Mastery. Already have SAP hands-on experience? Skip straight to the Training Library. The sequence is a recommendation, not a gate.

Not sure which door to use first?

If you've never reviewed a controls issue before, start with the No-Access Track. If you can already spot a segregation-of-duties conflict but haven't configured a fix, go straight to Mastery. If you're technically ready and job-hunting now, the Training Library is built for you.

New to controls review → No-Access Track Ready to configure in SAP → Mastery Console Ready to interview → Training Library
