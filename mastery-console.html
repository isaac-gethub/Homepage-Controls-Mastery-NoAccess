Background

Why this programme exists

Reading evidence and forming a judgment is one skill. Sitting inside SAP GRC and actually building the fix — a role, a ruleset, a risk analysis, a remediation plan — is a different one entirely, and it's the one most self-study courses skip because it requires a real system to practice on. Mastery Console is built around live SAP GRC labs, not screenshots, so every skill you practice is the exact skill a hiring manager tests for on day one of the job.

Choose your pace

Two tracks, one curriculum

85 sessions

Scope covered

What the curriculum actually spans

FOUNDATIONS

Business, SAP & ITGC Foundations

The access / change / operations framework, ITGC and ITAC basics, and how a Big 4-style audit actually approaches an SAP environment.

SOX 404

SOX Methodology & Controls Practice

Risk-based scoping, control design, sampling, testing methodology, and deficiency evaluation and classification.

GRC AC SUITE

SAP GRC Access Control — Live

Hands-on work across Access Risk Analysis (ARA), Access Request Management (ARM), Emergency Access (EAM), and Business Role Management (BRM).

RULESET & ROLES

Ruleset & Role Design

Building and troubleshooting a GRC ruleset and enterprise role design from the ground up, not just reviewing someone else's.

REPORTING

Deficiency & Audit Committee Reporting

Translating a technical finding into a business-risk narrative an Audit Committee or executive audience will actually act on.

CAREER SIM

Interview Simulation

Structured interview practice built directly from the same scenarios and templates used earlier in the course.

Course layout

Six manuals, one continuous programme

The console sequences six reference manuals into a single session-by-session path — you're never reading a manual in isolation; the console tells you exactly when to open each one.

MANUAL 1

Core Curriculum & Technical Foundations

The entry-level technical build: SAP fundamentals, ITGC structure, process controls, and core security concepts.

MANUAL 2

GRC Mastery Reference — 23 Modules

The full technical deep-dive across the SAP GRC Access Control suite: ARA, ARM, EAM, BRM, ruleset design, and enterprise role architecture.

MANUAL 3

Reference & Templates

Sixteen working templates — RCM, walkthrough documentation, PBC tracker, sampling workpaper, deficiency evaluation memo, remediation tracker, and Audit Committee deck outline among them.

MANUAL 4

SOX / ITGC Case Study Workbook

Five standalone case studies plus a full capstone simulation that threads every skill through one continuous SOX programme cycle at a fictional company.

MANUAL 5–6

Career & Interview Companion

Job market intelligence and an interview-preparation framework used most intensively in the final phase of the programme, before you move to the Training Library.
